What is Pollution? 🌎

Pollution (Verschmutzung) happens when harmful things make our air (Luft), water (Wasser) and surroundings dirty.

Let's meet three types of pollution and learn how we can help our planet!

1. Air Pollution (Luftverschmutzung) 🌫️

🏭 💨 🌳

What is it?

Air pollution happens when smoke (Rauch), dust (Staub) and harmful gases (schädliche Gase) make the air dirty.

📖 A Little Story: Sammy and the Dirty Air

Sammy the squirrel (Eichhörnchen) loved playing outside with his friends.

One day, he saw a factory (Fabrik) making lots of black smoke (schwarzer Rauch). Cars (Autos) were also making smoke.

The air became dirty, and Sammy started coughing (husten).

His friend Mia said, "Let's plant trees (Bäume)! Trees help clean the air!"

They planted little trees together. Sammy was happy because he was helping the Earth! 🌳

💚 How can we help?

  • Plant trees (Bäume pflanzen).
  • Walk or ride a bicycle (Fahrrad fahren).
  • Do not burn rubbish (Müll verbrennen).
  • Keep the air clean (Luft sauber halten).

2. Water Pollution (Wasserverschmutzung) 💧

🌊 🐟 🧴 🐢

What is it?

Water pollution happens when plastic (Plastik), rubbish (Müll) and harmful chemicals (schädliche Chemikalien) make water dirty.

📖 A Little Story: Benny the Little Fish

Benny was a little fish (Fisch) who lived in a beautiful blue river (Fluss).

He loved swimming with his friends.

One morning, Benny saw plastic bottles (Plastikflaschen) and rubbish floating in the water.

The water became dirty. Benny couldn't find a clean place to swim.

A kind little girl named Emma picked up the rubbish with help from an adult and put it in the bin (Mülleimer).

The river became cleaner, and Benny could swim happily again! 🐟

💚 How can we help?

  • Never throw rubbish into rivers (Flüsse).
  • Use fewer plastic bags (Plastiktüten).
  • Put rubbish in the bin (Mülleimer).
  • Keep oceans (Meere) clean.

3. Sound Pollution (Lärmverschmutzung) 🔊

🚗 📢 ✈️ 🔊 🐦

What is it?

Sound pollution happens when sounds (Geräusche) become too loud and disturb people and animals.

📖 A Little Story: Tina the Sleeping Bird

Tina was a little bird (Vogel) who lived in a beautiful tree.

Every morning, she loved listening to the quiet sounds of nature (Natur).

But one day, cars started honking (hupen), machines started working and people made lots of noise.

Tina couldn't sleep or hear her friends singing.

A little boy named Leo asked everyone to be quieter. They turned down the music (Musik) and stopped shouting (schreien).

Soon, Tina could hear the beautiful birds singing again! 🐦

💚 How can we help?

  • Keep music quiet (Musik leise machen).
  • Do not shout (nicht schreien).
  • Avoid unnecessary honking (unnötiges Hupen vermeiden).
  • Respect animals and people (Menschen und Tiere respektieren).
