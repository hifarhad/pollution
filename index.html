1. Air Pollution (Luftverschmutzung) 🌫️

🏭 💨 🌳

Air pollution happens when smoke (Rauch), dust (Staub) and harmful gases (schädliche Gase) make the air dirty.

📖 Sammy and the Dirty Air

Sammy the squirrel (Eichhörnchen) loved playing outside. One day he saw smoke coming from a factory (Fabrik) and cars (Autos).

The air became dirty. His friend Mia said: "Let's plant trees (Bäume)! Trees help clean the air!"

They planted trees together. Sammy was happy! 🌳

💚 How can we help?

  • Plant trees (Bäume pflanzen).
  • Ride a bicycle (Fahrrad fahren).
  • Keep the air clean (Luft sauber halten).

2. Water Pollution (Wasserverschmutzung) 💧

🌊 🐟 🧴 🐢

Water pollution happens when plastic (Plastik), rubbish (Müll) and harmful chemicals (schädliche Chemikalien) make water dirty.

📖 Benny the Little Fish

Benny was a little fish (Fisch) living in a beautiful river (Fluss).

One day he saw plastic bottles (Plastikflaschen) floating in the water.

A kind girl named Emma helped clean up the rubbish. Soon the river looked much better!

💚 How can we help?

  • Never throw rubbish into rivers (Flüsse).
  • Use fewer plastic bags (Plastiktüten).
  • Keep oceans (Meere) clean.

3. Sound Pollution (Lärmverschmutzung) 🔊

🚗 📢 ✈️ 🔊 🐦

Sound pollution happens when sounds (Geräusche) become too loud and disturb people and animals.

📖 Tina the Sleeping Bird

Tina was a little bird (Vogel) living in a tree. She loved the quiet sounds of nature (Natur).

One day, cars started honking (hupen) and machines became very noisy.

Tina couldn't hear her friends singing. A little boy asked everyone to be quieter. Soon Tina could hear the birds again!

💚 How can we help?

  • Keep music quiet (Musik leise machen).
  • Do not shout (nicht schreien).
  • Respect animals (Tiere respektieren).
